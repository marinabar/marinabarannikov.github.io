Unaite

Unaite is a federation of AI student associations from eight French institutions, among them École Polytechnique, HEC Paris, and Dauphine-PSL. My role is Head of Research. I launched and am in charge of the Unaite Paper Club, a seminar series where students meet scientists from Meta, Hugging Face, and other partner labs to discuss recent papers. The federation also organizes hackathons and conferences.
