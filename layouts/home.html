Unaite

Unaite is a federation of AI student associations from eight French institutions, among them École Polytechnique, Centrale Supéléc, HEC Paris, or Dauphine-PSL, where I am the Head of Research.

I launched the Unaite Paper Club, a seminar series where students meet scientists from Meta, Google Deepmind, Anthropic, and other partner labs and institutions to discuss recent papers. Unaite Paper Club #4 Stéphane D'Ascoli / Meta FAIR.

Unaite also organizes hackathons and conferences, and workshops, for example at VivaTech 2026 on AI security. Unaite recently launched a fellowship to support early-stage researchers in AI for Biology.
